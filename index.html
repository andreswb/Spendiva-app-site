Frequently Asked Questions

Does Spendiva work offline? Yes! All data is stored locally on your device. No internet connection is needed — ever. Your data never leaves your phone.
Can I have multiple trips? Yes. Create as many trips as you want. Tap Switch Trip in Settings to switch between them. Completed trips can be archived and reviewed any time.
How does currency conversion work? When creating a trip, you set the exchange rate between your home currency and the trip currency. The app uses this rate to convert all expenses. With Spendiva Pro you can also enter individual expenses in any currency with automatic conversion.
What does Spendiva Pro include? Pro unlocks: multi-currency expense entry, unlimited expense splitting (5+ people), corporate payment tracking, professional PDF reports, CSV export, and daily expense reminders. It's a one-time purchase with lifetime access — no subscription.
How do I split expenses? When entering an expense, use the people icons to split the cost. Tap 1–4 for quick selection, or tap + to enter a custom number. Splitting with 5 or more people requires Spendiva Pro.
Can I track business vs. personal expenses? Yes — Spendiva Pro includes a Corporate payment method that lets you separate business expenses from personal ones, making reimbursement tracking much easier.
How do I add details to an expense? Swipe left on any expense in the list to edit the amount, date, note, subcategory, or split count. Long-press a category when adding an expense to select a subcategory right away.
How do I backup my data? With Spendiva Pro, export to CSV or PDF from Settings. Exported files can be saved to your device or cloud storage. We recommend exporting regularly.
How do daily reminders work? Enable a daily notification in Settings → Daily Reminder. The reminder fires at your chosen time — but only on days when you have an active trip. It stops automatically when your trip ends, so you'll never receive reminders between trips. Requires Spendiva Pro.
What happens when I archive a trip? Archived trips are moved to a separate section and can no longer be selected as active. You can still view them, export their data, or restore them at any time.
Why is the note limited to 25 characters? The limit ensures notes display correctly in PDF exports and reports. Use concise descriptions like "La Trattoria" or "Museum ticket".
Does my Pro purchase work offline? Yes. Once verified, your Pro status is cached on your device and works fully in airplane mode.

Wie funktioniert die Währungsumrechnung? Beim Erstellen einer Reise legen Sie den Wechselkurs zwischen Ihrer Heimatwährung und der Reisewährung fest. Mit Spendiva Pro können Sie einzelne Ausgaben auch in beliebigen Währungen mit automatischer Umrechnung eingeben.
Was beinhaltet Spendiva Pro? Pro schaltet frei: Multi-Währungs-Ausgaben, unbegrenzte Ausgabenaufteilung (5+ Personen), Firmenzahlungsverfolgung, professionelle PDF-Berichte, CSV-Export und tägliche Erinnerungen. Einmaliger Kauf mit lebenslangem Zugriff.
Wie teile ich Ausgaben auf? Verwenden Sie beim Eingeben einer Ausgabe die Personensymbole. Tippen Sie 1–4 für Schnellauswahl oder + für eine benutzerdefinierte Anzahl. Das Aufteilen mit 5 oder mehr Personen erfordert Spendiva Pro.
Wie funktionieren die täglichen Erinnerungen? Aktivieren Sie unter Einstellungen → Tägliche Erinnerung eine Benachrichtigung. Sie wird nur an Tagen ausgelöst, an denen Sie eine aktive Reise haben — und endet automatisch, wenn die Reise endet. Erfordert Spendiva Pro.
