Frequently Asked Questions

Does Spendiva work offline? Yes! All data is stored locally on your device. No internet connection is needed — ever. Your data never leaves your phone.
Can I have multiple trips? Yes. Create as many trips as you want. Tap Switch Trip in Settings to switch between them. Completed trips can be archived and reviewed any time.
How does currency conversion work? When creating a trip, you set the exchange rate between your home currency and the trip currency. The app uses this rate to convert all expenses. With Spendiva Pro you can also enter individual expenses in any currency with automatic conversion. Pro also keeps exchange rates updated automatically from trusted sources like the European Central Bank; the free version uses dependable built-in rates you can edit by hand.
What does Spendiva Pro include? Pro unlocks: live exchange rates (auto-updated from the European Central Bank and other trusted sources), Trip Budget (set a spending limit per trip with a live tracker), multi-currency expense entry, unlimited group size on shared costs (5+ people), corporate payment tracking, professional PDF reports, CSV export, and daily expense reminders. It's a one-time purchase with lifetime access — no subscription.
How does the Trip Budget work? With Spendiva Pro, you can set a spending limit when creating or editing a trip. A live banner on the main screen then shows your total spent, remaining budget, and daily average — updating instantly as you log expenses. Perfect for staying on track during long trips or family holidays.
How do per-person costs on shared expenses work? When entering an expense, tap the people icons to set how many people the cost covers. Spendiva then shows the per-person amount automatically — both on screen and in your PDF report. Free plans cover groups up to 4 people; Spendiva Pro removes the group size limit.
Can I track business vs. personal expenses? Yes — Spendiva Pro includes a Corporate payment method that lets you separate business expenses from personal ones, making reimbursement tracking much easier.
How do I add details to an expense? Swipe left on any expense in the list to edit the amount, date, note, subcategory, or split count. Long-press a category when adding an expense to select a subcategory right away.
How do I backup my data? With Spendiva Pro, export to CSV or PDF from Settings. Exported files can be saved to your device or cloud storage. We recommend exporting regularly.
How do daily reminders work? Enable a daily notification in Settings → Daily Reminder. The reminder fires at your chosen time — but only on days when you have an active trip. It stops automatically when your trip ends, so you'll never receive reminders between trips. Requires Spendiva Pro.
What happens when I archive a trip? Archived trips are moved to a separate section and can no longer be selected as active. You can still view them, export their data, or restore them at any time.
Why is the note limited to 25 characters? The limit ensures notes display correctly in PDF exports and reports. Use concise descriptions like "La Trattoria" or "Museum ticket".
Does my Pro purchase work offline? Yes. Once verified, your Pro status is cached on your device and works fully in airplane mode.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Data Collection

Spendiva does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All expense data, trip information, and settings are stored exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

No Account Required

Spendiva requires no account, login, or registration of any kind. You can use all features without providing any personal information.

Local Storage Only

All data (trips, expenses, settings, preferences) is stored locally on your device. It is never uploaded to any server, shared with third parties, or synchronized to the cloud.

In-App Purchases

Spendiva Pro is processed entirely through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. We do not handle, store, or access any payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs all purchase transactions.

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders (Pro feature), Spendiva uses iOS's local notification system. No data is sent externally — notifications are scheduled entirely on your device.

Exchange Rates (Pro)

With Spendiva Pro, the app fetches up-to-date exchange rates from third-party providers such as the European Central Bank and exchangerate-api.com. These requests retrieve public rate data only and never include any personal, trip, or expense information. The free version works fully offline using built-in rates.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

Spendiva does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or crash reporting SDKs. No usage data is collected or sent anywhere.
